FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Which ABB equipment do you supply for data centers?

Medium and low voltage switchgear, transformers, UPS, automatic and static transfer switches, generator paralleling, busway, power conditioning and surge protection.

Do you do the data center's electrical engineering?

Yes. We handle conceptual, basic and detailed engineering, as well as supply, installation, commissioning and maintenance.

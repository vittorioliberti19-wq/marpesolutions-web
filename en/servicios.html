SERVICES

Design, supply, maintenance, repair, upgrading, analysis, testing, commissioning and operation. Four lines covering the full cycle.

01

Engineering

  • Development of technical specifications.
  • Conceptual, basic and detailed engineering for high, medium and low voltage electrical projects.
  • DC and AC system design for substation auxiliary services.
  • Design of solutions to increase production capacity in the oil and gas industry.
  • Protection coordination, short-circuit and load-flow studies, analysis of electrical variables, motor starting and restarting.

02

Transformers

  • Manufacturing, refurbishment, maintenance and servicing of power transformers.
  • Accredited laboratories in Colombia for physical-chemical testing, dissolved gas analysis by gas chromatography and oxidation inhibitor content analysis.
  • Field crew for preventive and corrective maintenance.
  • TTR, insulation, winding resistance, power factor and functional electrical testing.

03

Distribution and auxiliary services

  • Manufacturing of medium and low voltage switchboards, power houses and compact substations.
  • Manufacturing of DC and AC systems for auxiliary services.
  • Earthing systems for transmission lines, distribution networks and substations.
  • Technical assistance during installation, setup and start-up of high, medium and low voltage equipment.
  • Upgrading of electrical protections and implementation of new schemes.
  • Updating and preparation of electrical system drawings.

04

LV switchboard and MV switchgear maintenance

  • Technical support during installation, pre-commissioning and commissioning.
  • Point-to-point wiring checks on control, metering, protection and auxiliary circuits.
  • Closing and opening tests.
  • Current and voltage injection testing to verify tripping and metering circuits.
  • Pole simultaneity and closing/opening time testing.
  • Insulation measurement on power cables and busbar systems.
